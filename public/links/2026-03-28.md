## Summary
Build OpenClaw step by step from first principles: gateway connecting AI agent to messaging apps, computer tools, and persistent memory.

## The Problem with ChatGPT/Claude
- **Stateless** - every conversation starts from zero
- **Passive** - you go to it, it never comes to you
- **Isolated** - can't run commands, browse web, control apps
- **Single-channel** - lives in browser tab, not where you communicate

## OpenClaw Solution
- Lives in messaging apps you already use (WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord, etc.)
- Remembers preferences, projects, past conversations
- Runs commands on your computer, browses web, controls browser
- Wakes on schedule for recurring tasks
- Runs on your own hardware (always on, under your control)

## Architecture Components
- Messaging API gateway
- LLM integration
- Tool system (computer control, web, browser)
- Persistent sessions
- Multi-channel coordination
- 24/7 reliability

## Why It Matters
Understanding how persistent AI assistants work so you can build your own.
