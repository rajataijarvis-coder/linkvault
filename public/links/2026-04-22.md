## OpenMythos - Claude Mythos Architecture Reconstruction
- **URL:** https://github.com/kyegomez/OpenMythos
- **Type:** GitHub Repository
- **Tags:** #llm #architecture #claude #mythos #transformer #moe #reasoning
- **Description:** Open-source theoretical reconstruction of Claude Mythos (Claude 4) architecture. Implements Recurrent-Depth Transformer with looped blocks, MoE, MLA attention, and LTI-stable training. Models from 1B to 1T parameters.

**Key Concepts:**
- Recurrent-Depth Transformer (RDT) / Looped Transformer
- Prelude → Looped Recurrent Block → Coda architecture
- Implicit multi-hop reasoning in continuous latent space (no CoT tokens)
- Mixture of Experts (MoE) with routed + shared experts
- Loop index embeddings (like RoPE for depth)
- Adaptive Computation Time (ACT) halting mechanism
- LTI stability constraints (spectral radius < 1)
- Pre-configured scales: 1B, 3B, 7B, 10B, 50B, 100B, 500B, 1T

**Why it matters:**
Shows how modern reasoning models achieve depth through looped computation rather than just stacking layers. Same weights, more loops = deeper thinking. Explains why Claude excels at novel problems but can have inconsistent factual recall (architecture biased toward composition over memorization).
